Privacy policy

How we use personal data on the Expert Energy website and contact channels.

1. Who is responsible

Expert Energy Perfomance em Energia Ltda., registered under CNPJ 16.640.933/0001-83, in Ribeirão Preto, São Paulo, is responsible for decisions about the processing of personal data received to handle inquiries.

For privacy matters and to exercise your rights, contact contato1@expertenergy.com.br, with the subject “Privacy — Expert Energy”. This is our privacy contact channel; you do not need to purchase our services to use it.

2. What data we use and why

The guided assessment asks for your full name, company, job title, business email, phone/WhatsApp, sector, goal and a description of your needs. Depending on your answers, it also asks about energy procurement, project stage, equipment of interest and, optionally, your monthly bill range.

This information provides context for the request, directs technical support, helps answer questions, assess solutions and prepare proposals when requested. Name, company, job title, email, phone, description and authorization are required in the guided process. Without them, you can still browse the site or contact us directly.

We do not request sensitive personal data, identity documents, passwords or payment details in this form. Avoid including third-party information, sensitive data or confidential documents in the description field.

3. Grounds for processing

The authorization requested in the form is specific to analyzing your request and contacting you about that inquiry. It does not authorize promotional campaigns unrelated to the request.

Depending on the context, processing may also involve preliminary steps requested by the data subject before entering a contract, contract performance, compliance with legal or regulatory obligations, and the exercise of legal rights. Processing for security and prevention of misuse must respect applicable legal requirements and data subject rights.

You may withdraw consent through the privacy channel. Withdrawal does not invalidate previous lawful processing or prevent retention permitted or required by law.

4. How the form works

While you complete the assessment, your answers stay in the memory of the page. The site does not automatically send them to a server, save them in local storage or maintain a form database. Reloading or closing the page ends this temporary state.

  • Email: the button prepares a message in your configured email application. The application receives the content for review; you confirm sending it to Expert Energy.
  • WhatsApp: when you open this channel with the summary, the data is included in the access URL and transferred to the Meta service to prepare the conversation, even before you confirm sending the message to our team.
  • Copy summary: the content is placed on the device clipboard, subject to the settings and other applications on that device.
  • Microsoft Forms: technical documentation requests use an external form. Information entered there is processed in the Microsoft environment and received to handle the request.

After you send us a message or submit an external form, the information becomes part of the corresponding contact channels. These records are not deleted when you close this website.

5. Hosting, cookies and external services

The public website is served by GitHub Pages, with delivery and security services from Cloudflare. These providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, access dates and times, browser information, requests and security logs.

The code in this version does not install advertising tools, social media pixels or audience analytics, or create its own cookies for those purposes. This does not mean technical logs or essential infrastructure and external-service cookies are absent.

External Google Fonts only load after you authorize them in the notice or preferences panel. This service receives technical connection information. Without authorization, the site uses device fonts. Links to LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook lead to external services and are not embedded feeds; their policies and settings apply when you access them.

Email, WhatsApp and Microsoft Forms have their own privacy and security rules. Consult the information provided by each service before using these channels.

How we remember your choice

The notice lets you agree to external fonts, continue with essentials only or configure your preferences. No optional category is active before authorization. Your choice does not authorize marketing campaigns or replace the specific authorization requested in the form.

We store the notice and policy version, selected option, authorized category, record date and expiry in the local storage of this browser for up to 180 days. This record does not contain form answers and is not sent to a server by the site code.

You can change or withdraw permission using “Cookie preferences” in the footer. Withdrawing permission removes external font loading for subsequent use of the site; it does not erase data previously received by the provider. Clearing browser data or using another device may make the notice reappear. If the browser prevents storage, the choice applies only to the current page.

6. Sharing and international access

Inquiry information is intended for the people who need to analyze and answer the request and for providers required to operate the chosen channels. It may also be disclosed to authorities under a legal obligation or valid request. We do not authorize the sale of data provided in the assessment.

Hosting, font, email, form and messaging providers may operate infrastructure or teams in other countries. Where applicable, international processing must comply with the conditions and safeguards set out in the LGPD and ANPD regulations. Request clarification about a specific inquiry through the privacy channel.

7. Retention and security

The form on the page is temporary. After receipt through an external channel, retention depends on the purpose of the inquiry, any contractual relationship, legal and regulatory obligations, and the exercise of rights. Information without a purpose justifying retention should be deleted or anonymized, except where retention is legally permitted or required.

Access and sharing must be limited to what is necessary for these purposes. No digital service can guarantee absolute security. If you suspect misuse of your data connected to this site, notify the privacy channel. Relevant incidents will be handled in accordance with applicable legal duties.

8. Your rights

Under the LGPD, you may request confirmation and access to your data; correction; information about sharing; anonymization, blocking or deletion where applicable; portability under applicable regulations; deletion of data processed with consent, subject to legal exceptions; and withdrawal of consent. You may also challenge processing and request review of solely automated decisions affecting your interests, where applicable.

The assessment on this site only suggests a topic for discussion based on the selected answers. It does not replace specialist evaluation or decide on contracting or eligibility.

Send your request to contato1@expertenergy.com.br. We may request proportionate information to confirm your identity and protect the data, without asking for unnecessary documents. Exercising your rights is free; responses will follow applicable legal deadlines and conditions.

If our response does not resolve your request, you may contact the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD) and, where applicable, consumer protection authorities. Read ANPD guidance on data subject rights.

9. Audience and updates

This site presents business solutions and is not directed at children. If data about children is submitted inappropriately, notify us so we can assess the appropriate handling.

This policy applies to the corporate website and the contact channels described here. Independent platforms and other services may have their own policies. We will update this document when relevant changes occur in site operation or data processing, with the revision date shown on this page.
